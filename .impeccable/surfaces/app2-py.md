Scope: Career Path page (`app2.py`), the first product surface for users starting a career exploration.

Mode: Operate. The page must help a player or agent enter the current football context and understand what Kickways will do next.

Audience and job: semi-professional/amateur players and agents asking, "If I play here today, where could my career realistically go next?"

Chosen direction: Command Brief. The first viewport behaves like a compact career intelligence console: a decisive question, an explicit journey route, an input station, and an evidence briefing.

Primary task: select current country and league, then run "Find realistic opportunities."

Proof/content: transfer count, player count, historical next moves, Opta level movement, and the promise of destination intelligence reports.

Constraints: keep the established dark SaaS Kickways system, one blue accent, plain language, DuckDB-backed data, and no invented claims.
