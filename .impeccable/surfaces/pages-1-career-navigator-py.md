Scope: Opportunity Explorer / Career Navigator page (`pages/1_Career_Navigator.py`), the profile-based exploration surface after the main Career Path entry.

Mode: Operate. The page must help a player or agent tune a comparable-player profile, scan career opportunities, and inspect the players behind a destination signal.

Audience and job: players and agents who need to move from a broad current context to specific historical examples and destination evidence.

Chosen direction: Profile Workbench. The first action is building the cohort; the main body behaves like an opportunity desk with destination cards on the left and a player inspector on the right.

Primary task: adjust profile filters, review ranked destinations, open destination intelligence, or reveal comparable players and their Transfermarkt profiles.

Proof/content: comparable-player counts, recorded transfers, onward level movement, evidence strength, player examples, and Transfermarkt links.

Constraints: preserve Kickways' dark SaaS system, one blue accent, plain-language decision copy, DuckDB-backed data, and metric consistency with destination reports.
